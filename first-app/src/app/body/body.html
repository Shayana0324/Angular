Read a Book!

Love getting lost in a good book? So do we! Read a book is a private book club in Forsyth County that meets every Wednesday to chat about our latest read, exchange favourite quotes and insights, and connect with fellow readers.It's the perfect place to discover new reads, make genuine connections, and be part of a growing community of people who simply love books.

    What we offer?
  • Weekly book discussions.
  • A welcoming and inclusive community.
  • Opportunities to meet fellow book lovers.
  • Improve critical thinking and communication skills.
  • Explore books you might not choose on your own.
    How It Works?
  1. Join the club.
  2. Read at your own pace before the meeting.
  3. Meet every Wednesday with the group.
  4. Share your thoughts, favourite moments, and insights.
  5. Vote or suggest books for future meetings.
  6. Repeat with a new book and new discussions!
